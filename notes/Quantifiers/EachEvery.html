Each & Every (Determiners)

Prerequisites to understand first:
  • Singular and Plural Nouns (Ek vachan vs Bahu vachan)
  • Subject-Verb Agreement (Singular subject takes singular verb)
  • Countable vs Uncountable Nouns (Jise gin sakte hain vs nahi gin sakte)

1. Core Meaning & Difference

Dono ka matlab "har ek" hota hai par context alag hai.

  • EACH: Jab hum individual identity (alag-alag) par focus karte hain. Use for 2 or more things.
    Example: Each hand has five fingers. (Dono haath alag-alag dekhe ja rahe hain).
  • EVERY: Jab hum group ko as a whole (ek sath) dekhte hain like 'all'. Use for 3 or more things.
    Example: Every student must wear a uniform. (Puri class ki baat ho rahi hai).

2. Grammar Rules (Mind Map)

  • Rule A (Always Singular): "Each" aur "Every" hamesha singular countable noun aur singular verb lete hain.
    ✅ Each boy is... | ❌ Each boys are...
  • Rule B (Each of): "Each of the" ke baad noun Plural aata hai lekin verb Singular hi rehti hai.
    ✅ Each of the boys is... | ❌ Each of the boy is...
  • Rule C (No Every of): "Every of" English grammar mein completely wrong hai. Iski jagah "Every one of" use karein.
  • Rule D (Adverbs with Every): Almost practically nearly jaise words sirf "Every" ke sath aate hain "Each" ke sath nahi.
    ✅ Almost every car... | ❌ Almost each car...
  • Rule E (Abstract Nouns): "Every" abstract nouns (feelings reasons) ke sath lag sakta hai. "Each" nahi.
    ✅ He had every reason to be angry.

3. Top Mistakes People Make

Mistake 1: Plural Verb Lagana
"Each of my friends are going" bolna galat hai. "Each" pure sentence ko singular bana deta hai.
Right: Each of my friends IS going.
Mistake 2: Only 2 things ke liye Every lagana
"He held a bag in every hand" galat hai kyunki insaan ke 2 hi haath hote hain aur Every minimum 3 ke liye lagta hai.
Right: He held a bag in each hand.

Contextual Story

It was Diwali season and every house in our colony was decorated with lights. My mother gave us a list of tasks. She told us that each person had to finish their chores before noon. Each of my siblings took a different room to clean.

I went to the market. Almost every shop was crowded. I bought five boxes of sweets. I made sure that each box contained fresh laddoos. The shopkeeper smiled and said he checks each sweet carefully.

By evening every member of the family was exhausted. But each face had a bright smile. We lit diyas in every corner of the house holding a diya in each hand carefully.

Analysis: Notice how "every house" talks about the whole colony as a group. But "each person" and "each box" focuses on the specific individual item. "Each hand" is used because a person has exactly two hands.
