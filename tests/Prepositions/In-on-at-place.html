1. The Preposition 'IN' (Enclosed Spaces)

Basic Logic: Jab koi cheez kisi 3D space, area, ya boundaries ke andar ho, tab hum 'IN' use karte hain. Socho jaise koi cheez ek box ke andar band hai.

  • Rooms/Buildings: I am in the room. (Room ek enclosed jagah hai)
  • Cities/Countries (Bade areas): He lives in Mumbai. She is in India. (Bade borders ke andar)
  • Containers/Liquids: The keys are in my pocket. The fish is in the water.

2. The Preposition 'ON' (Surfaces)

Basic Logic: Jab koi cheez kisi flat ya vertical surface ko touch kar rahi ho aur uske upar tiki ho. Boundary nahi hai, bas contact hai.

  • Flat Surfaces: The book is on the table. The picture is on the wall.
  • Streets/Roads: The shop is on MG Road. (Road ek lambi line/surface ki tarah treat hoti hai)
  • Public Transport: I am on the bus / train / plane. (Logic: Agar aap us gaadi mein khade hokar chal sakte hain, toh 'ON' lagta hai).

3. The Preposition 'AT' (Specific Points)

Basic Logic: Jab hum ek exact point, location ya aisi jagah ki baat karein jise hum nakshay (map) par ek dot ki tarah dekhte hain.

  • Specific Locations: I am waiting at the bus stop. Someone is at the door.
  • Events/Group activities: We met at a party. He is at the concert.
  • Addresses: She lives at 45 Park Street. (Exact number pata hai toh At, warna sirf street ho toh On).

Key Nuances & Logical Differences

"In the hospital" vs "At the hospital"
Agar koi mareez (patient) ki tarah admit hai: "He is in the hospital."
Agar aap kisi se milne gaye hain (visitor): "I am at the hospital."
Vehicles (Car vs Bus)
Jin gadiyon mein aapko jhuk kar ghusna pade aur chal na sakein (Car, Taxi, Helicopter) -> IN (In a car).
Jin gadiyon ki chat unchi ho aur chal sakein (Bus, Train, Airplane) -> ON (On a bus).
Corner (In vs At)
Kamre ka kona (enclosed): In the corner of the room.
Sadak ka kona (point): At the corner of the street.

Top Mistakes Indian Speakers Make

  • ❌ I live at Mumbai. ✅ I live in Mumbai. (City bada area hai, specific point nahi).
  • ❌ I am in the bus. ✅ I am on the bus. (Bus mein chal sakte hain).
  • ❌ Put the pen in the table. ✅ Put the pen on the table. (Table surface hai, uske andar nahi daal rahe).
  • ❌ Someone is knocking on the door. ✅ Someone is knocking at the door. (Door ek location point ki tarah use ho raha hai).
