Prepositions of Direction & Movement

Prerequisite Concept: To fully grasp this, you should know the basics of Prepositions of Place. Learn Prerequisites on Google.

1. The Basic Concept (Manzil vs Disha)

TO (The Destination / Manzil)
'To' ka istemaal tab hota hai jab aapki manzil (destination) fix ho. Iska matlab hai ki movement us specific jagah par khatam hogi. Agar aap 'to' use kar rahe hain, to iska implied meaning hai ki aap us jagah pahuch gaye ya pahuchege.

Logical Example: "I am going to the station."
Kyu? Kyunki yaha 'station' hi aakhri manzil hai. Main station par hi ja kar rukunga.

TOWARDS (The Direction / Disha)
'Towards' ka istemaal tab hota hai jab aap kisi cheez ki disha (direction) mein ja rahe ho, par zaroori nahi ki wahi aapki aakhri manzil ho. Destination is not guaranteed.

Logical Example: "I am going towards the station."
Kyu? Kyunki main bas station ke raaste par chal raha hu. Ho sakta hai main raste mein kisi dukaan par ruk jau. Manzil station nahi, bas uski disha hai.

2. Structural Mind Map

  • 🎯 TO (End Point / Goal)
    • • Shows a specific destination (He drove to London).
    • • Shows receiving something (Give the book to me).
    • • Shows attachment (Attach the file to the email).
  • 🧭 TOWARDS (Journey / Path)
    • • Shows physical direction (She looked towards the window).
    • • Shows relation/attitude (His attitude towards his work is bad).
    • • Shows progression in time/goal (We are moving towards a solution).

3. Top Mistakes & Nuances

❌ Mistake 1: Handing over objects

"I gave the keys towards him."

✅ "I gave the keys to him."

Reasoning: Jab aap kisi ko kuch dete ho, toh wo insaan final receiver (destination) hota hai, isliye 'to' aayega.

❌ Mistake 2: Physical orientation without movement

"He turned his face to the wall." (Usually unnatural unless he touched it)

✅ "He turned his face towards the wall."

Reasoning: Chehra ghumana ek disha (direction) batata hai. Chehra deewar se jaa kar takraya nahi (destination nahi hai).

🔍 Nuance: Feelings & Attitudes

Emotions aur attitudes ke liye hamesha 'towards' use hota hai.

Example: "He is aggressive towards me."

'To me' yaha galat sound karega kyunki emotion ek flow ya disha hai, physical object delivery nahi.

4. Key Questions Answered

Q: Can they be used interchangeably?

A: Kabhi nahi. "I ran to the river" ka matlab tum nadi tak pahuch gaye. "I ran towards the river" ka matlab tum bas us disha me bhage, shayad beech me hi ruk gaye.

Q: British vs American English me koi farq hai?

A: Haan! British English mein "towards" use hota hai. American English mein aksar bina 's' ke "toward" use hota hai. Dono ka matlab bilkul same hai.

A Walk in the City

Rahul woke up early. He had an interview. He got dressed and headed to the bus stop. The bus stop was his destination.

While waiting, he saw a stray dog walking towards him. The dog didn't stop near him; it just kept walking in his direction and eventually passed by.

He took the bus to the city center. Once he got off, he realized he was lost. He turned towards a traffic police officer to ask for directions.

"You need to go to the blue building," the officer said, pointing towards the east. The officer's finger indicated the direction, but the blue building was the final goal.

Rahul smiled. His attitude towards the day was positive. He finally walked to the office, pushed the door, and stepped inside.
