Parts of Speech Sentence Structure

1. What is an Interjection?

Interjection grammar ka sabse chhota hissa (part of speech) hai. Yeh aisi aawazein ya chhote words hote hain jo achanak aayi hui feelings (emotions) ko express karte hain—jaise khushi surprise dukh ya gussa.

  • Yeh words sentence ka main hissa nahi hote.
  • Inhe hata dene se bhi sentence ke meaning par grammatical farq nahi padta sirf emotion kam ho jata hai.
  • Example: Wow that car is fast! (Agar 'Wow' hata dein toh bhi "that car is fast" ek poora sentence hai).

2. Why are they their own part of speech?

Aap soch sakte hain ki in chote words ko alag part of speech kyun maante hain? Iske 2 main reasons hain:

  • Rule-breakers: Yeh English grammar ke placement aur sentence structure rules ko bilkul disregard (ignore) karte hain. Subject verb ya object se inka koi direct connection nahi hota.
  • Starting Position: Yeh almost hamesha sentence ki starting mein aate hain kyuki emotion pehle nikalta hai uske baad hum logic ya baat bolte hain.
Logical Example: "Ouch my foot hurts." Yahan 'Ouch' bas pain dikha raha hai. Iska 'my foot' (subject) ya 'hurts' (verb) se koi grammatical relation nahi hai. Isliye isey independent part of speech maana jata hai.

3. Deep Dive into Common Interjections

Ah

Use: Relief realization ya samajh aane ka expression.

Ah ab mujhe samajh aaya ki machine kaise chalti hai.

Cool

Use: Agreement ya approval dikhane ke liye (often informal).

Cool main kal wahan pahunch jaunga.

Aha

Use: Sudden discovery ya kisi ki chori pakadne par.

Aha toh tumne khaya tha mera pizza!

Damn

Use: Frustration shock ya kabhi-kabhi extreme impress hone par.

Damn traffic kitna zyada hai aaj!

Oh

Use: Surprise realization ya just a filler word.

Oh mujhe nahi pata tha tum aa rahe ho.

mmm / Ooh

Use: 'mmm' sochne ya taste ke liye. 'Ooh' fascination ya interest ke liye.

mmm yeh biryani bahut achhi hai. Ooh nayi gadi!

Ouch

Use: Sudden physical ya emotional pain.

Ouch mera pair table se takra gaya!

Uh

Use: Hesitation bolne se pehle atakna ya confuse hona.

Uh mujhe lagta hai hum rasta bhatak gaye hain.

Wow / Yippee / Yuck

Wow: Amazement. Yippee: Extreme joy. Yuck: Disgust (ghinn aana).

Yuck is sabzi mein bahut namak hai!

4. Top Mistakes People Make

Mistake 1: Punctuation error

Log aksar strong interjection ke baad full stop laga dete hain. Wrong: Ouch. It hurts. Right: Ouch! It hurts. ya Ouch it hurts.

Mistake 2: Overusing in formal writing

Emails ya formal essays mein 'Uh' 'Wow' ya 'Damn' use karna unprofessional lagta hai. Inhe sirf spoken English ya casual chats mein rakhein.

A Morning of Interjections

Ah the sun is finally up. I stretched my arms feeling the warmth through the window. Ooh the coffee smells amazing today I thought as I walked into the kitchen.

Suddenly my brother walked in carrying a weird looking green smoothie. "Yuck what is that?" I asked backing away.

"It is kale and ginger" he said. "Cool" I replied sarcastically "keep it away from me."

Just then my phone buzzed. I looked at the text and gasped. "Damn the train is cancelled!" I yelled. "Oh no how will you get to college?" my brother asked looking worried.

"Uh I guess I will have to take the bus." I rushed to grab my bag but stubbed my toe on the table. "Ouch!" I hopped on one foot. "Aha" my brother smirked "that is karma for insulting my smoothie."

I glared at him and rushed out. I made it to the bus stop right as the bus arrived. "Wow that was lucky!" I panted. Finding an empty seat I sat down heavily. "mmm" I hummed "at least the day is getting better."

Then I saw my friend waving from the back of the bus. "Yippee" she mouthed happily. I smiled back. Maybe today would not be so bad after all.
