1. Introduction - Samay ka Khel

English mein jab bhi hume waqt (time) batana hota hai, hum generally 3 choti words ka use karte hain: At, In, On. Inhe Prepositions of Time kehte hain. Indian speakers aksar inme confuse hote hain kyunki Hindi mein hum sab jagah "mein" ya "par" use kar lete hain (jaise: Monday ko, May mein, 3 baje par). English bohot specific hai.

Dimag mein set karne ki trick (The Triangle Rule):

  • IN = Sabse bada samay (Years, Months).
  • ON = Usse chota samay (Specific Days, Dates).
  • AT = Sabse chota aur precise samay (Ghadi ka waqt).

2. AT - The Precise Sniper

"At" ka use tab hota hai jab aap kisi ekdum fix point ya ghadi ke time ki baat kar rahe ho. Socho "At" ek sniper hai jo exact point par target karta hai.

  • Clock Times (Ghadi ka waqt): at 5 o'clock, at 10:30 PM, at noon, at midnight.
    Ex: Main usse at 4 PM milunga.
  • Precise Moments (Khaas pal): at sunrise, at sunset, at present, at the moment.
    Ex: Woh at the moment busy hai.
  • Festivals/Holidays (Poora festival period, bina 'day' word ke): at Diwali, at Christmas.
    Ex: Hum at Diwali ghar jayenge (Diwali ki chhuttiyon mein).

3. IN - The Big Container

"In" ek baday container jaisa hai. Jab waqt thoda lamba ho (mahine, saal, sadiyaan), tab 'in' lagta hai. Waqt ek duration hai jiske 'andar' kuch ho raha hai.

  • Months, Years, Centuries: in August, in 2026, in the 21st century.
    Ex: India got independence in 1947.
  • Seasons (Mausam): in winter, in the summer.
    Ex: Hum in winter Manali jayenge.
  • Parts of the Day (Din ke hisse): in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening.
    Ex: Main in the morning exercise karta hoon.
    LOGICAL EXCEPTION: Raat ke liye humesha "at night" hota hai, "in the night" sirf tab jab kisi specific ek khaas raat ki baat ho.

4. ON - Days and Dates

"On" ka rule sabse simple hai. Agar baat din (Day) ki ho rahi hai ya Tareekh (Date) ki, toh aankh band karke 'On' laga do.

  • Days of the week: on Monday, on Sundays, on my birthday.
    Ex: Mera exam on Friday hai.
  • Dates (Tareekhein): on 15th August, on May 3rd.
    Ex: Salary on the 1st aati hai.
  • Specific Days with parts: on Monday morning, on Friday evening.
    Reasoning: Kyunki pehla word 'Monday' (Day) aa gaya, toh rule 'On' ka lagega, chahe aage morning likha ho.

5. Top Mistakes & Nuances (Dhyan Rakhein!)

Mistake 1: Last, Next, Every, This ke sath prepositions lagana

Jab bhi aap last, next, every, this bolte ho, toh At, In, On nahi lagate.

❌ I went to Delhi in last May.

✅ I went to Delhi last May. (Correct)

Nuance: In Time vs On Time

  • On time: Bilkul fix time par, na jaldi na late. (Punctual).
    Ex: The train arrived on time at 9:00 AM.
  • In time: Waqt rehte huye, ya waqt se thoda pehle.
    Ex: I reached the station in time to buy tickets. (Matlab train chhutne se pehle pahunch gaya).

Nuance: At the end vs In the end

  • At the end: Kisi specific cheez ka aakhiri hissa (requires 'of').
    Ex: At the end of the movie.
  • In the end: Aakhirkaar, final conclusion (Finally).
    Ex: We had a lot of problems, but in the end, everything was fine.

Rohan was born in 1995. He works as a software engineer. His typical day starts early. He wakes up at 6:00 AM in the morning.

His manager told him, "We have a project deadline in May, so we need to work hard." Rohan agreed. He promised to submit the initial report on 15th April.

He left the office at sunset. In the evening, he watched a movie. He finally went to bed at midnight. He usually doesn't work on weekends, but he might have to log in on Sunday morning to check some codes.

He is planning to go to Goa in winter. He loves the beach at night. He hopes to book the tickets at the moment.
