1. Prerequisites

Before mastering Have/Has/Had, you must understand:

  • Subjects: Singular (He, She, It) vs Plural (I, You, We, They). Google Search ↗
  • Verb Forms: V1, V2, V3. (e.g., Go, Went, Gone). Google Search ↗

2. Possession (Adhikar ya Rishta)

Jab dikhana ho ki koi cheez aapki hai ya kisi aur ki hai.

  • Have: I, You, We, They ke saath. (Present)
    Ex: I have a car. (Mere paas car hai.)
  • Has: He, She, It ke saath. (Present)
    Ex: Rahul has a problem. (Rahul ko ek dikkat hai.)
  • Had: Sabhi ke saath PAST mein.
    Ex: They had a house in Mumbai. (Unke paas Mumbai mein ghar tha.)

3. Perfect Tenses (Jo kaam poora ho chuka ho)

Formula: Subject + Have/Has/Had + V3 (Third form of verb)

  • Present Perfect: Koi kaam abhi haal hi mein khatam hua hai.
    Ex: I have eaten my lunch. (Maine apna lunch kha liya hai.)
  • Past Perfect: Past mein kisi aur kaam se pehle koi kaam ho chuka tha.
    Ex: The train had left before I reached the station. (Mere station pahunchne se pehle train ja chuki thi.)

4. Compulsion / Obligation (Majboori)

Jab koi kaam karna padta hai (majboori ya duty).

  • Have to / Has to: Present majboori.
    Ex: I have to wake up early. (Mujhe jaldi uthna padta hai.)
  • Had to: Past majboori.
    Ex: I had to pay a fine. (Mujhe fine bharna pada.)

⚠️ Top Mistakes

❌ Did you had your dinner?
✅ Did you have your dinner?

Reason: 'Did' khud past tense hai. Ek sentence mein do past markers (did + had) nahi aate. Did ke baad hamesha base form (have) aata hai.

❌ He have a new bike.
✅ He has a new bike.

Reason: He, She, It (Singular 3rd person) ke saath hamesha 'has' lagta hai, 'have' nahi.

❌ I had went to market.
✅ I had gone to market.

Reason: Have/Has/Had ke baad hamesha Verb ki 3rd form aati hai (gone), na ki 2nd form (went).

A Busy Morning

Rohan has a very strict boss. Because of this, he has to reach the office by 9 AM every day. Yesterday, he had a terrible morning.

He woke up and realized he had slept through his alarm. "I have missed the train," he thought. He had to take an auto to the station.

When he reached the platform, the train had already left. He had to wait twenty minutes for the next one. During the journey, he remembered that he hadn't had his breakfast.

By the time he reached the office, his boss had already started the meeting. His boss looked at him and said, "You have been late three times this month. We have to discuss your punctuality."

Now, Rohan has decided that he will buy a second alarm clock because he doesn't have any excuse left.
