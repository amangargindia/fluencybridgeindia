During vs. While

Table of Contents

  • 1. Prerequisites (Pehle kya pata hona chahiye)
  • 2. Core Concept & Rules
  • 3. Logical Mind Map
  • 4. Top Mistakes
  • 5. Detailed Examples

1. Prerequisites

Is topic ko samajhne ke liye ye concepts clear hone chahiye:

  • Nouns: Kisi cheez, jagah ya event ka naam (e.g., the movie, summer, the meeting).
  • Clauses (Subject + Verb): Jisme ek doer (subject) ho aur ek action (verb) ho (e.g., I was sleeping).

2. Core Concept (Hinglish)

Dono "During" aur "While" ka matlab hota hai "us dauran" ya "jab kuch chal raha tha". Par inko use karne ka rule ekdum alag hai.

RULE 1: During

During + Noun (Event/Time)

Jab aap kisi 'event' ya 'noun' ka zikr karte hain (bina kisi action verb ke), toh DURING lagta hai.

✅ During the movie
✅ During summer

RULE 2: While

While + Subject + Verb

Jab aap puri baat bolte hain jisme koi subject action kar raha ho, tab WHILE lagta hai. (Ya direct Verb+ing ke saath).

✅ While I was watching
✅ While eating

3. Logical Breakdown (Mind Map)

  • Action ho raha hai? (Verb in progress)
    • Haan, pura sentence hai (Subject + Verb) → Use WHILE (e.g., While she was singing)
    • Haan, par sirf action hai (Verb+ing) → Use WHILE (e.g., While singing)
  • Sirf Event ka naam hai? (Noun, No verb)
    • Haan → Use DURING (e.g., During the concert)

4. Top Mistakes

  • ❌ Mistake 1: Using 'during' before a verb.
    Wrong: I fell asleep during watching the movie.
    Right: I fell asleep while watching the movie. (Kyunki 'watching' verb hai).
  • ❌ Mistake 2: Using 'while' before a pure noun event.
    Wrong: The phone rang while the meeting.
    Right: The phone rang during the meeting.

5. Real Examples Analysed

Scenario 1: Jab chuha bike me ghus raha tha, tab Himanshu bhaiya laddoo kha rhe the.

Translation 1 (While): Himanshu bhaiya was eating laddoo while the rat was entering into the bike.
Logic: "the rat was entering" ek clause hai (Subject + verb), isliye 'while' use hua.

Translation 2 (During): Himanshu bhaiya was eating laddoo during the time when the rat was entering into the bike.
Logic: 'During' direct clause ke pehle nahi lagta, isliye "the time when" jodna pada noun phrase banane ke liye.

Scenario 2: Jab aap mujhe bula rhe the, tab mai padh rhi thi.

Translation: I was studying while you were calling me.
Logic: "you were calling" clause hai.

Scenario 3: Jab chori hui, tab mai so raha tha.

Translation: I was sleeping while the theft happened.

Scenario 4: Historical Context

Translation: World War 2 was happening while India was fighting for its freedom.
Yaha do lambe actions ek sath chal rahe hain.

A Day in the Life

It was a busy Tuesday. During the morning meeting, my boss asked for the reports. I was so stressed because my laptop restarted while I was presenting the slides.

While waiting for it to turn on, I tried to make small talk. Thankfully, during the break, I managed to fix the issue.

Later that day, I went out for lunch. While I was eating my sandwich, it started raining heavily. Many people rushed into the cafe during the downpour.

In the evening, while driving back home, I listened to a podcast. The traffic was terrible during the rush hour. I finally reached home, and while unlocking the door, I realized I had left my keys inside the office!

Observation:

  • Notice how "during" is always followed by a noun (the meeting, the break, the downpour, the rush hour).
  • Notice how "while" is always followed by an action (I was presenting, waiting, I was eating, driving, unlocking).
