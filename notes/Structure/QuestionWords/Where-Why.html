1. Introduction to Why & Where

English grammar mein questions banane ke liye hum "Wh-family" words ka use karte hain. Is module mein hum do sabse important words seekhenge:

  • Why (Kyun): Yeh humesha Reason (karan) puchne ke liye use hota hai.
  • Where (Kahan): Yeh humesha Place (jagah) ya location puchne ke liye use hota hai.
Prerequisites (Pehle kya pata hona chahiye?) Is topic ko master karne ke liye aapko in do cheezon ka basic knowledge hona chahiye:
1. Auxiliary (Helping) Verbs (do/does/is/am/are/was/were) 2. Subject-Verb Inversion (Question banate waqt verb pehle aata hai)

2. Question Structure (Formula)

Sabse badi galti log structure mein karte hain. English mein direct question ka ek universal formula hota hai:

Wh-Word + Helping Verb + Subject + Main Verb + Object?
  • Right: Where (Wh-word) are (Helping verb) you (Subject) going (Main verb)?
  • Wrong: Where you are going? (Kyunki helping verb 'are' subject 'you' ke baad lagaya hai, jo statement hai, question nahi)

3. Where (Kahan) - Nuances

"Where" sirf ek point nahi, balki direction ya origin bhi bata sakta hai.

  • Location (Jagah): Where is my phone? (Mera phone kahan hai?)
  • Destination (Manzil): Where are we going? (Hum kahan ja rahe hain?)
  • Origin (Kahan se): Where are you from? (Aap kahan se hain?) -> Note: End mein 'from' lagana ek common conversational style hai.

4. Why (Kyun) - Nuances

"Why" actions ka reason mangta hai. Iske answers aksar 'Because' ya 'To' se shuru hote hain.

  • Direct Reason: Why are you crying? (Tum ro kyun rahe ho?)
  • With Negative (Kyun nahi): Why didn't you tell me? (Tumne mujhe bataya kyun nahi?) -> Structure: Why + Helping Verb + Not + Subject...

5. Top Mistakes

Mistake 1: Missing Helping Verb

Log aksar sentence ko direct translate karte hain.

Why you late?
Why are you late?

Mistake 2: Did ke sath V2 use karna

Agar 'Did' aa gaya, toh main verb hamesha V1 (base form) hoga.

Why did you went?
Why did you go?

Story: The Lost Keys

Rohan was getting late for his office. He looked at the clock and panicked. He started searching his room.

"Where are my bike keys?" he shouted.

His mother came into the room. "Why are you shouting in the morning, Rohan?" she asked calmly.

"Because I can't find my keys, Ma! Where did I put them last night?" he replied, throwing clothes off his bed.

"Why don't you keep your things in their proper place?" she scolded gently. "Where did you go yesterday evening?"

"I went to the grocery store. Wait... why is my jacket on the sofa?" Rohan asked himself. He ran to the living room and checked the jacket pockets.

He smiled. "Why do I always forget to take them out of my pocket?" he laughed. "Found them, Ma!"

"Good. Now, where is your helmet?" she asked, smiling.

Rohan froze. "Why does this keep happening to me?"
