Concept: Double Apostrophe 's'

English grammar mein, jab hum do nouns ka possession (adhikar) ek sath dikhana chahte hain, toh hum aksar galti se do baar 's laga dete hain. Jaise: "Aman's car's window". Yeh technically aur stylistically incorrect maana jata hai.

Rules & Logic

  • Rule 1: Avoid chaining inanimate objects.
    Non-living things (jaise car, phone, ghar) technically kuch "own" nahi karte. Isliye unke sath 's ka use awkward lagta hai.
  • The Fix: Use "The [Noun 3] of [Noun 1]'s [Noun 2]"
    ❌ Incorrect: Rahul's phone's screen is broken.
    ✅ Correct: The screen of Rahul's phone is broken.

    Logic: Humne last object (screen) ko pehle rakha aur use "of" se connect kiya "Rahul's phone" ke sath.

Nuances (Gehrai ki baatein)

Kya double 's hamesha galat hota hai? Nahi.

  • Agar dono nouns living beings (insaan/jaanwar) hain, toh log daily bhasha mein iska use kar lete hain, halanki formal writing mein abhi bhi isse bachte hain.
    Example: "My brother's friend's dog." (Acceptable informal English).
  • Lekin agar ek bhi non-living cheez beech mein aati hai, toh rule strictly apply hota hai.
    Example: "My brother's company's policy" ko "The policy of my brother's company" likhna zyada behtar aur professional hai.

Top Mistakes Indians Make

  • Mistake: "India's economy's growth" bolna.
    Correction: "The growth of India's economy".
  • Mistake: "Sir's car's keys" bolna.
    Correction: "The keys to Sir's car" (Keys ke sath 'to' zyada natural lagta hai 'of' ki jagah).

A Clumsy Day

Yesterday, I borrowed my friend's car to attend a wedding. I didn't want to use my own vehicle because the engine of my car was making a strange noise. I parked outside the venue, but in my hurry, I accidentally slammed the door too hard. Suddenly, a crack appeared on the window of Aman's car!

I panicked. I quickly took out my phone to call him, but in my nervousness, it slipped. The screen of my phone shattered on the pavement. Now I had two broken things to worry about.

When Aman arrived, he wasn't mad about the car. He looked at my device and laughed, saying, "Well, replacing the display of your mobile is going to cost more than fixing the glass of my window." It was a terrible day for fragile things, but a great day for our friendship.

Notice: The story used "the engine of my car" instead of "my car's engine", and "the window of Aman's car" instead of "Aman's car's window". This makes the writing flow beautifully without sounding clunky!
