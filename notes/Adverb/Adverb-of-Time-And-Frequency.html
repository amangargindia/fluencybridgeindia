1. Prerequisites & Basics

Is topic ko samajhne se pehle aapko Verbs (Kriya) ka basic idea hona chahiye. Verb woh word hota hai jo koi action dikhata hai (jaise play eat sleep). Adverb woh word hota hai jo Verb ke baare mein aur extra information deta hai. Aaj hum do specific types padhenge: Time aur Frequency.

2. Adverbs of Time (Waqt)

Yeh adverbs humein batate hain ki koi action KAB (When) hua. Inhe pehchanne ka sabse aasan tarika hai sentence se "Kab?" poochna.

  • Examples: today tomorrow yesterday now then soon later tonight.
  • Rule of Placement: Adverbs of time zyadatar sentence ke end mein aate hain.
  • Logical Example: "I will call you tomorrow." (Main tumhe kal call karunga). Yahan 'tomorrow' end mein aaya hai.
  • Why at the end? English mein pehle hum batate hain 'Kaun kya kar raha hai' (Subject + Verb + Object), uske baad hum batate hain ki woh kab ho raha hai.

3. Adverbs of Frequency (Kitni Baar)

Yeh adverbs humein batate hain ki koi action KITNI BAAR (How often) hota hai. Kya woh roz hota hai kabhi kabhi hota hai ya kabhi nahi hota?

  • Examples: always (hamesha) usually (aksar) sometimes (kabhi kabhi) rarely (shayad hi kabhi) never (kabhi nahi).
  • Rule of Placement (Very Important):
    1. Main verb se pehle aate hain. (I always eat apples).
    2. Agar sentence mein 'to be' verb (is/am/are/was/were) hai toh uske baad aate hain. (I am always late).
  • Why this placement? Frequency dikhane wale words action se itne jude hote hain ki woh action word (verb) ke bilkul saath aana pasand karte hain.

⚠️ 4. Top Mistakes People Make

Mistake 1: Placing Frequency at the end.

Wrong: I go to the gym always.

Right: I always go to the gym.

Reason: Always frequency batata hai isliye main verb (go) se pehle aayega.

Mistake 2: Messing up with "is/am/are".

Wrong: He always is late.

Right: He is always late.

Reason: Helping verb (is) humesha frequency adverb ko apne baad rakhta hai.

Mistake 3: Putting Time between Subject and Verb.

Wrong: I today will buy a car.

Right: I will buy a car today.

Reason: Time adverbs usually sentence ke end mein set hote hain.

5. Concept Mind Map

Adverbs
↳ Time (Kab?) -> End of sentence
- Past: yesterday previously
- Present: now today
- Future: tomorrow soon
↳ Frequency (Kitni baar?)
- Before Main Verb: I never lie.
- After 'Be' Verb: I am never late.
- Words: always usually often sometimes rarely never.

A Day in Rohan's Life

Notice how adverbs of time (yellow) and frequency (blue) are used naturally in this simple story.

Rohan always wakes up early. Today he woke up at 6 AM. He usually drinks a cup of chai first. He is never lazy in the morning.

He will go to the office later. He often takes the metro because it is fast. He rarely takes a bus because the bus is always crowded.

Yesterday it rained heavily. Rohan was late for his meeting. His boss is sometimes strict but he understood the situation then.

Now Rohan is working on his laptop. He will finish his project soon. He always feels happy when he goes home.
