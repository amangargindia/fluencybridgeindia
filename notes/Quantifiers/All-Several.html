Meaning & Core Logic

"All" ka matlab hota hai "sab kuch" ya "saara". Yeh 100% quantity ko dikhata hai. Chaiye wo log ho (sab log) ya paani ho (saara paani).

"Several" ka matlab hota hai "kayi" ya "kuch se zyada, par bohot saare nahi". Agar 10 log hain, toh 4-5 log 'several' kehlayenge. Yeh humesha ek se zyada (plural) ke liye use hota hai.

Grammar Rules

How to use "All"

  • Rule 1: All + Plural Countable Noun + Plural Verb Logic: Agar ginn sakte ho, toh sab mila kar plural ban jayenge. Ex: All the students are present. (Saare bachhe present hain)
  • Rule 2: All + Uncountable Noun + Singular Verb Logic: Jise ginn nahi sakte (like water, information), wo group as a whole singular treat hota hai. Ex: All the water is spilled. (Saara paani gir gaya hai)

How to use "Several"

  • Rule 1: Several + Plural Countable Noun + Plural Verb Logic: Several ka matlab hi "kayi" hota hai (more than 2), toh aage ka noun aur verb humesha plural hi hoga. Ex: Several people were waiting. (Kayi log wait kar rahe the)
  • Rule 2: NEVER use with Uncountable Nouns Logic: Aap "kayi paani" ya "kayi information" nahi bol sakte. Ex: Several waters are there.

Top Mistakes People Make

  • All the information are wrong.
    All the information is wrong.

    Kyu? Information uncountable hai. Isliye iske saath singular verb ('is') aayega, chahe hum 'All' use kar rahe ho.

  • I need several sugar.
    I need some sugar / a lot of sugar.

    Kyu? Sugar ko count nahi kar sakte (1 sugar, 2 sugar nahi bolte). 'Several' sirf countable cheezon ke liye bana hai.

  • Several boy is playing.
    Several boys are playing.

    Kyu? Several hamesha plural (ek se zyada) hota hai. Toh Noun aur Verb dono plural ('boys', 'are') hone chahiye.

The Wedding Preparation

All the family members were gathered in the living room to discuss the upcoming wedding. There was so much to do, and all the time they had seemed to be slipping away quickly. Ravi looked at the guest list. "Have we invited all the relatives?" he asked. His mother nodded, mentioning that several of them had already booked their train tickets from Mumbai.

The caterer arrived later that afternoon. He presented a menu, and several dishes caught everyone's attention immediately. "We need to make sure all the food is vegetarian," Ravi's father instructed. The caterer assured them that all the ingredients used would be fresh. While discussing sweets, they decided to order several boxes of Kaju Katli, because all the kids loved it.

By night, all the major decisions were finalized. Ravi was exhausted. He had made several phone calls to decorators and vendors. Even though there were still several minor tasks left, he felt relieved. He knew that when the big day arrived, all their hard work would pay off.

Observation:

  • Notice how "All the family members" (plural countable) takes "were".
  • Notice how "All the time" (uncountable) takes "seemed" (singular context) or "All the food" takes "is".
  • Notice how "several" is always followed by plurals: "several dishes", "several boxes", "several phone calls".
