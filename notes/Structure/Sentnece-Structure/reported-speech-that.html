2. 'That' kya hai aur kyun use hota hai?

Jab koi insaan kuch kehta hai, aur hum uski kahi hui baat kisi aur ko batate hain, toh hum dono baaton ko jodne ke liye "that" ka use karte hain. "That" ka Hindi meaning yahan "ki" hota hai.

Original Words: Rahul said, "I am busy."

Sharing the info: Rahul said that he was busy. (Rahul ne kaha ki woh busy tha.)

3. Mind Map: Rules of using 'That'

  • Rule 1: 'That' is mostly Optional

    Aam bolchaal (Spoken English) mein hum "that" ko chhod bhi sakte hain. Dono sahi hain.

    • ✔️ He told me that he was coming. (Formal/Written)
    • ✔️ He told me he was coming. (Informal/Spoken - Totally correct)

  • Rule 2: Statements vs. Questions

    Hum 'that' sirf normal baaton ke liye lagate hain. Sawaalon (Questions) mein 'that' KABHI nahi lagta.

    • ❌ She asked me that where I was. (Galat)
    • ✔️ She asked me where I was. (Sahi - Wh-word khud link banta hai)

  • Rule 3: Time and Person Change

    Jab hum 'that' lagakar purani baat jodte hain, toh andar ka Time aur Insaan badal jata hai.

    • "I will call you," she said.
    • -> She said that she would call me. (I ban gaya she, will ban gaya would)

4. Top Mistakes (Aksar hone wali galtiyan)

Mistake 1: "That" ke sath "If/Whether" lagana

Log aksar bolte hain: "He asked that if I am okay." Yeh galat hai. Question ko share karte time sirf 'if' ya 'whether' lagta hai. "That" sirf normal baaton ke liye hai.

Mistake 2: "Told to" ya "Said me" likhna

Telling words theek se use karein.
❌ "He said me that he is busy." (Galat)
✔️ "He told me that he was busy." (Sahi) ya "He said that he was busy."

5. Nuances (Chhoti par zaroori baatein)

Agar koi aisi baat boli gayi hai jo Universal Truth hai (jo humesha sach hoti hai) ya jo aadat hai, toh 'that' ke baad aane wale hisse ka time (past/present) nahi badalta.

Teacher said, "The earth is round."

-> The teacher said that the earth is round. (Was round nahi hoga kyunki earth abhi bhi round hai!)

The Office Mix-Up

Read this story. Notice how characters share what others said using "that".

It was a chaotic Monday morning at the Mumbai office. Rohan rushed to Anjali's desk and said that the boss was extremely angry. Anjali frowned. She replied that she had already submitted the report on Friday.

Just then, the HR manager walked in. He announced that there would be a surprise inspection today. Hearing this, Rohan whispered to Anjali that they needed to clean their desks immediately.

Later in the cafeteria, Anjali told her friend Sneha that the boss had yelled at Rohan. Sneha was shocked. She mentioned that Rohan had been working very hard lately. Sneha also added that it wasn't fair to shout at employees.

By evening, the boss called a meeting. He clarified that he wasn't angry at anyone; he was just stressed about a client. He assured the team that their jobs were safe. Rohan sighed in relief and thought to himself that he had panicked for no reason.
