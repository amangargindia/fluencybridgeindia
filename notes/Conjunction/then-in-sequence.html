Revise: "Then" in Sequence

Prerequisites (Pehle yeh aana chahiye):

  • Connecting Words (Joining words like 'and', 'but')
  • Time Sequence Words (Words showing 'when' something happened)
  • Comma Splices & Run-on Sentences

1. Core Concept (Asli funda kya hai)

English mein 'Then' ek aisa word hai jo time ya order batane ke kaam aata hai. Iska main matlab hota hai "us waqt" ya "uske baad" (next in order). Yeh actions ka sequence batane ke liye sabse zyada use hota hai.

  • For Sequence: "First I boiled the water. Then, I added the tea leaves." (Pehle paani ubala. Uske baad chai patti daali.)
  • For 'At that time': "I was living in Delhi then." (Main us waqt Delhi mein reh raha tha.)
  • For Logic (If-Then): "If it rains, then we will stay home." (Agar barish hui, toh hum ghar rahenge.)

2. Top Mistakes (Sabse zyada hone wali galtiyan)

Mistake A: Gluing Sentences Only With 'Then' (Sabse badi galti)

'Then' mein akele do complete sentences ko jodne ki power nahi hoti. Ise directly use karne se sentence ka structure galat ho jata hai aur ek run-on sentence ban jata hai.

❌ Incorrect: I ate dinner then I went to sleep.

✅ Correct: I ate dinner, and then I went to sleep.

✅ Correct: I ate dinner. Then, I went to sleep.

✅ Correct: I ate dinner; then, I went to sleep.

Logic: Kyunki 'then' akele do alag ideas ko nahi chipka sakta, usko apne sath 'and' ki zarurat hoti hai, ya phir dono sentences ko full stop/semicolon lagakar alag karna padta hai.

Mistake B: Rambling Stories (Bina soche sequence batana)

Stories likhte waqt baar-baar "then... then... then" use karne se sentence childish lagta hai aur story ki flow kharab hoti hai.

❌ Rambling: He woke up then he brushed his teeth then he went to school then he met his friends.

✅ Better Flow: After waking up, he brushed his teeth. Later, he went to school, where he met his friends.

Tip: Sequence batane ke liye transitions use karein jaise: Afterwards, subsequently, following that, later, next.

3. Structure Mind-Map

  • ● Main Role: Showing Time & Order
  • └─ Used for Sequence (Next)
  • └─ Used for Time (At that moment)
  • └─ Used for Condition (If X, then Y)
  • ● Limitation: Cannot glue two independent sentences together alone

Story Time: The Morning Rush

Notice how sequence is established without overusing the word "then". When "then" is used, observe the punctuation around it.

The alarm buzzed aggressively at 6:00 AM. Rohan smashed the snooze button instinctively, hoping for just five more minutes of peace. Then, realizing he had an important presentation, he leaped out of bed. First, he rushed to the kitchen to start the coffee maker. While it brewed, he quickly ironed his shirt.

He grabbed his mug, took a burning sip, and then scrambled to find his car keys. They weren't on the table. If they weren't on the table, then he must have left them in his jacket pocket last night. He checked the coat rack, found them, and dashed out the door.

Later at the office, he successfully delivered his pitch. His boss nodded approvingly. Only then did Rohan finally let out a sigh of relief. The frantic morning was worth it.
