Adverb of Manner

Master how actions are performed.

Revision Notes

Prerequisites

Is topic ko samajhne ke liye aapko inka basic idea hona chahiye:

  • Verbs (Kaam / Action words)
  • Adjectives (Khasiyat batane wale words)

Ye kya hota hai? (What is it?)

Adverb of Manner humein batata hai ki koi kaam (verb) KAISE (how) hua. Ye action ka tarika (manner) batata hai.

  • Example 1: He runs quickly. Wo bhagta hai. Kaise bhagta hai? Quickly (tezi se). 'Quickly' yahan adverb of manner hai.
  • Example 2: She sings beautifully. Wo gaati hai. Kaise gaati hai? Beautifully (khoobsurti se).

Top Mistakes (Kahan galti hoti hai?)

1. 'Fastly' word nahi hota!

Log sochte hain 'fast' ka adverb 'fastly' hoga. Lekin English mein 'fastly' exist nahi karta. Adjective aur Adverb dono 'fast' hi rehte hain.

He runs fastly. He runs fast.

2. 'Good' vs 'Well'

'Good' ek adjective hai (noun ki khasiyat batata hai). Kaam kaise hua ye batane ke liye (adverb) 'well' use hota hai.

He speaks English good. He speaks English well.

3. 'Hard' vs 'Hardly'

'Hard' ka matlab hota hai 'mehnat se' ya 'zor se'. 'Hardly' ka matlab bilkul alag hai - iska matlab hota hai 'mushkil se' ya 'na ke barabar'.

Wo mehnat karta hai: He works hardly. He works hard.

Story Time

The Midnight Storm

The wind howled fiercely outside the small cabin. Rain beat heavily against the glass windows. Inside, old Thomas sat quietly by the fireplace.

His dog, Max, slept soundly on the rug. Suddenly, a branch crashed loudly onto the roof. Thomas stood up quickly.

He walked carefully to the window and looked out. He could hardly see anything through the darkness. He closed the curtains tightly and waited patiently for the storm to pass. He knew he had built the cabin well.
