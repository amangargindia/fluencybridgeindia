1. Basic Rule (Golden Logic)

SINCE = "Kab Se" (Starting Point)

Jab aapko wo exact point batana ho jahan se kaam SHURU hua tha. Aap ghadi ya calendar mein uspe ungli rakh sakte ho.

  • • Time: Since 9 AM since 8 o'clock.
  • • Days/Dates: Since Monday since 4th July.
  • • Years/Months: Since 2015 since January.
  • • Events/Life phases: Since childhood since my wedding since I woke up.

FOR = "Kitni Der Se" (Duration)

Jab aap total samay (length of time) ki ginti batate ho. Isme starting point nahi diya hota bas total time diya hota hai.

  • • Minutes/Hours: For 5 minutes for 2 hours.
  • • Days/Weeks: For 3 days for a week.
  • • Months/Years: For 6 months for 10 years.
  • • Vague times: For a long time for ages for a while.

3. Nuances & Traps (Dhokha kahan hota hai)

  • Trap 1 "For a long time" vs "Since a long time"

    Log aksar kehte hain "Since a long time". Ye GALAT hai. "A long time" ek lamba samay (duration) hai isliye hamesha "For a long time" bole.

  • Trap 2 Clauses with Since

    Aap Since ke baad puri line (clause) likh sakte ho jo starting event ka kaam karegi. Example "I have known him since we were in school." Yahan "we were in school" ek past event hai.

4. The Tense Mistake (Very Important)

Preposition sahi lagane se kaam nahi chalega Tense bhi badalna padta hai

❌ Wrong

"I am living here since 2010."

Reason 'am living' present continuous hai. Ye us kaam ke liye hai jo ABHI chal raha hai usme 'since/for' nahi jodte.

✅ Right

"I have been living here since 2010."

Rule Jab bhi time duration/start point do hamesha Present Perfect Continuous (have/has been + ing) use karo.

Daily Conversations

Examples strictly relatable for Indian middle-class scenarios.

🛵

Zomato/Swiggy Delivery

"Bhaiya I have been waiting outside the gate for 20 minutes. Kahan ho aap?"

Why 'for'? Because 20 minutes is a duration.

📚

Exam Preparation (JEE/UPSC)

"Wo ladka Kota mein since 10th class coaching kar raha hai."

Why 'since'? 10th class is a specific phase/event in life acting as a start point.

🏠

Renting / Society Talk

"Hum toh is society mein since 2012 reh rahe hain tab toh yahan paani ki dikkat nahi thi."

Why 'since'? 2012 is a specific calendar year.

⚡

Power Cut / Inverter Issues

"Subah se light nahi hai. Inverter has been running since morning."

"It has been off for 6 hours."

Notice the flip? Morning is a point (since). 6 hours is the counted duration (for).
