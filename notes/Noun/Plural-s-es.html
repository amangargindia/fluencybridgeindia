2. The Hissing Theory

English mein 90% words ko plural (bahu vachan) banane ke liye sirf end mein 's' lagana padta hai (jaise book -> books). Lekin kuch words mein 'es' lagta hai. Aisa kyun?

Iska reason hai Pronunciation (Bolne ka tareeqa). Agar kisi word ke last mein pehle se hi hissing sound (saanp jaisi sssss ya shhh) nikal rahi hai toh uske aage ek aur 's' lagana gale ke liye mushkil hota hai. Try saying "bus-s" or "box-s". Ajeeb hai na? Isliye hum usko smooth banane ke liye 'es' lagate hain jiska sound 'iz' nikalta hai. "Bus-iz" (buses). Isko The Hissing Theory kehte hain.

3. Logical Rules & Mind Map

  • Rule 1: Normal Words (No Hiss) Jin words ke end mein aam aawaz hoti hai wahan bas s lagayein.
    Examples: Car -> Cars | Pen -> Pens | Table -> Tables
  • Rule 2: The Hissing Sounds (Add 'es') Agar word in letters pe end hota hai: s, ss, sh, ch, x, z toh es lagayein.
    - s / ss : Bus -> Buses | Class -> Classes
    - sh : Dish -> Dishes | Brush -> Brushes
    - ch : Watch -> Watches | Match -> Matches
    - x : Box -> Boxes | Fox -> Foxes

4. Top Mistakes People Make

Mistake 1: Hissing sound na hone par bhi 'es' lagana.

Bookes are on the table.

Books are on the table.

Kyun? 'k' sound hissing nahi hai aaram se 's' lagaya ja sakta hai.

Mistake 2: 'x' wale words mein sirf 's' lagana.

I have two boxs.

I have two boxes.

Kyun? 'x' se hissing sound aati hai uske theek baad 's' bolna physical taur pe throat ke liye mushkil hai. Isliye syllable break ke liye 'es' (iz) chahiye.

Story Time: A Busy Market Day

Notice how normal words just take an 's' while words that end with hissing sounds (ch sh x ss) take 'es' to make pronunciation smooth.

On Sunday morning I took two buses to reach the market. The streets were filled with cars and bikes.

My first stop was a shop selling beautiful watches. I did not buy any because I only had a few rupees. Next I bought some fresh apples and put them in different bags.

As I walked further I saw a shop selling kitchen items. I bought two glasses for water and three new dishes for my mother. The shopkeeper packed them safely into small cardboard boxes.

By noon I had completed all my tasks. I saw two foxes painted on a wall mural near the station before heading home to rest before my evening classes.
