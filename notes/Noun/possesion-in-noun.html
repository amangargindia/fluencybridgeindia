1. Prerequisites

Ye topic start karne se pehle aapko pata hona chahiye ki Singular (Ek vachan) aur Plural (Bahu vachan) Nouns kya hote hain. Aapko ye pehchanna aana chahiye ki koi word living (jaan daar) hai ya non-living (bezubaan/nirjeev).

2. Core Concept

Jab hume dikhana hota hai ki "kiska kya hai" (Ownership ya relation - jaise "Rahul ki gaadi" ya "Ghar ki chhat"), humare paas English mein do main tareeqe hote hain: 's (Apostrophe S) ya of.

Rule A: Living Things

Insaano aur jaanwaro (Living things) ke liye humesha 's ka use karein.

Rahul's car (Rahul ki gaadi) The car of Rahul (Awkward lagta hai)

Rule B: Non-Living

Bejaan cheezo (Non-living things) ke liye humesha of ka use karein.

The screen of the phone The phone's screen (Grammatically galat)

3. Plurals & Words ending in 'S'

Sabse zyada confusion tab hoti hai jab word ke end mein pehle se hi 's' laga ho (Plural hone ki wajah se ya naam ki spelling ki wajah se).

  • 1
    Plural Nouns ending in 's' Agar word plural hai aur end mein 's' aa raha hai (jaise boys, girls, teachers) - Toh sirf apostrophe ( ' ) lagate hain, extra 's' nahi.

    Right: The boys' hostel (Ladkon ka hostel)
    Wrong: The boys's hostel (Galat aur bolne mein ajeeb)
  • 2
    Plural Nouns NOT ending in 's' Kuch plural words 's' se end nahi hote (jaise men, women, children). Inme normal 's lagta hai.

    Right: Children's park (Bacchon ka park)
    Wrong: Childrens' park
  • 3
    Singular Names ending in 's' Agar kisi ka naam 's' se end hota hai (jaise James, Vikas, Boss), toh modern grammar ke hisaab se aap dono use kar sakte hain, par 's zyada clear maana jaata hai.

    Option 1: Vikas's bike (Zyada common aaj kal)
    Option 2: Vikas' bike (Ye bhi theek hai)

4. Top Mistakes People Make

Mistake 1: Using 's for non-living objects.

The table's legs are broken. The legs of the table are broken.

Kyun? Table ek bejaan cheez hai. Iske saath "of" structure use karna chahiye.

Mistake 2: Getting confused with "Womens" or "Mens".

Mens clothing section. Men's clothing section.

Kyun? 'Men' already plural hai 'man' ka. Isliye isme normal 's lagta hai ownership dikhane ke liye.

Story Time: Vikas's First Day

Notice how apostrophes and "of" are used below. Living things own things via 's. Non-living objects relate via "of". Plurals ending in 's' just take an apostrophe (').

It was Vikas's first day at the new college. He packed his mother's homemade lunch into his bag.

When he reached the campus, he admired the color of the building. He couldn't say "the building's color" because the building is non-living. He walked towards the boys' hostel to drop off his luggage. Since "boys" is plural and ends in 's', he knew it only needed an apostrophe at the end.

Later, he visited the teachers' lounge to submit a form. On his way back, he noticed the wheel of his bicycle was flat. "Not a great start," he thought. He borrowed Amit's pump to fix it.

In the evening, he went to a shop to buy some things. He went to the men's section to buy a shirt. Because "men" doesn't end in 's', the section is written with a standard 's. By the end of the day, he was tired but happy.
