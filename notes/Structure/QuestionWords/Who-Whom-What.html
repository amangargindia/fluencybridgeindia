Who, Whom & What

Prerequisites (Click to search)

  • Subject (Doer) & Object (Receiver)
  • Pronouns (He/Him, She/Her, They/Them)
  • Prepositions (to, for, with, about)

1. The Core Concept (Hinglish)

English mein log aksar "Who" aur "Whom" ko lekar confuse hote hain. Iska ek simple rule hai: Subject vs Object.

  • WHO (Kaun - Subject): Yeh action karne wale (Doer) ke liye use hota hai. Agar sentence ka answer "He", "She", ya "They" hai, toh "Who" lagega.
    Ex: Who is knocking? (Knock kaun kar raha hai?) -> He is knocking. (Isliye Who)
  • WHOM (Kisko / Kise - Object): Yeh uske liye use hota hai jis par action ho raha hai (Receiver). Agar answer "Him", "Her", ya "Them" hai, toh "Whom" lagega.
    Ex: Whom should I call? (Main kisko call karu?) -> Call him. (Isliye Whom)
  • WHAT (Kya - Subject/Object for things): Yeh non-living things, ideas, ya actions ke liye use hota hai. Yeh subject aur object dono ban sakta hai.
    Ex: What fell on the floor? (Subject). What do you want? (Object).

2. The "Preposition" Trick

Jab bhi sentence mein preposition (to, with, for, about) aata hai jiske baad person aana ho, hamesha Whom use hota hai, Who nahi.

  • Galat: With who are you going?
  • Sahi: With whom are you going? (Kiske saath ja rahe ho?)
  • Sahi: To whom it may concern.

3. Top Mistakes People Make

  • Mistake 1: Using "Who" after a preposition. (e.g., "For who is this gift?" -> should be "For whom").
  • Mistake 2: Using "Whom" as a subject to sound smart. (e.g., "Whom is at the door?" -> should be "Who is at the door?").
  • Mistake 3: Confusing "What" with "Which". "What" is open-ended (Kya chahiye?), "Which" implies a limited choice (Kaunsa wala chahiye?).

The Missing Wedding Gift

It was Rahul's wedding day. His mother looked stressed and asked, "Who is bringing the sweets?" (Kaun la raha hai? - Subject)

Rahul replied, "I don't know who has the boxes, but I will find out." He then turned to his brother and asked, "Whom did you invite from the office?" (Tumne kisko invite kiya? - Object)

"I invited the manager, but I don't know with whom he is coming," his brother said. (Kiske saath aa raha hai? - Preposition + Object)

Suddenly, a loud crash was heard from the kitchen. "What was that sound?" Rahul's mother yelled. (Kya tha? - Subject for a thing)

Rahul ran in. "I dropped the tray. What should I do now?" (Main kya karu? - Object for an idea/thing). His mother sighed, "Find the person to whom the catering is assigned and ask him to clean it up."
