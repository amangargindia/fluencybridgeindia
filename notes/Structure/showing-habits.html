1. The Concept

Used to ka istemaal hum past ki un aadaton (habits) ya states (situations) ko batane ke liye karte hain jo pehle sach thi, par ab nahi hain. Simple shabdon mein, "Main aisa kiya karta tha" ya "Main aisa hua karta tha".

  • Past Habit: I used to play cricket. (Main cricket khela karta tha - ab nahi khelta).
  • Past State: I used to live in Delhi. (Main Delhi mein raha karta tha - ab nahi rehta).

3. Sentence Structures

Affirmative (Positive)

Subject + used to + Verb(1st form)

Example: We used to travel by train. (Hum train se safar kiya karte the.)

Negative

Subject + did not use to + Verb(1st form)

Example: I didn't use to like karela. (Mujhe karela pasand nahi hua karta tha.)
Logic: 'did' already past tense hai, isliye hum 'used' nahi, sirf 'use' likhte hain.

Interrogative (Question)

Did + Subject + use to + Verb(1st form)?

Example: Did you use to watch Shaktimaan? (Kya tum Shaktimaan dekha karte the?)

4. Top Mistakes & Nuances

  • ❌

    I didn't used to go.

    Did ke baad hamesha base form aati hai. Correct: I didn't use to go.

  • ❌

    I was used to go to school.

    "was used to" ka matlab habit hona hota hai, par iske baad Verb+ing aata hai. Agar past routine batana hai toh sirf: I used to go to school.

  • ❌

    I use to wake up early now.

    Present habits ke liye "use to" nahi lagate. Simple present tense use hota hai. Correct: I wake up early now.

Reading: A 90s Childhood

When I was young, life used to be much simpler. We didn't have smartphones or high-speed internet. Every evening, all the kids in our neighborhood used to gather in the street to play cricket. We didn't use to worry about traffic because there were hardly any cars. During summer vacations, my entire family used to travel to my grandmother's village by train. I used to love sitting by the window, feeling the wind on my face. My grandmother used to make the most delicious laddoos for us. We used to sleep on the terrace under the stars because there didn't use to be frequent electricity at night. Now, everything has changed. But I often smile when I think about the things we used to do.
