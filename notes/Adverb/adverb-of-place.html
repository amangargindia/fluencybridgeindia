What is Adverb of Place?

Sentence mein jo word ye batata hai ki action kahan (where) hua hai, usko Adverb of Place bolte hain.

Contrast to remember:
"Kal ki thi" (I went to the market yesterday/last year/this evening) -> Ye Adverb of Time hai kyunki ye Kab (When) ka answer deta hai.
Adverb of Place hamesha Kahan (Where) ka answer dega.

Example:

  • My pen is there. (Mera pen vaha hai)
  • My pen is on the table downstairs. (Mera pen niche table par hai)
  • The person is inside the box. (Wo insaan box ke andar hai)

Vocabulary & Nuances (Mind Map View)

  • Here / There (Yaha par / Vaha)

    Directly points to a location.

    Ex: The rat is here. (Chuha yaha hai)

  • Outside / Inside (Baahar / Andar)

    Used for boundaries and enclosures.

    Ex: My pencil is outside the box/almirah.

    Nuance check

    "I am going outside" = Direction (Mai bahar ki taraf ja raha hu)

    "I am outside" = Current Position (Mai bahar khada hu)

  • Everywhere / Nowhere (Har jagah / Kahi bhi nahi)

    Used for sweeping or non-existent locations.

    Ex: Air is everywhere. My earphones are nowhere.

  • Upwards (Upar ki taraf)

    Specifically shows direction of movement.

    Ex: The pot is thrown upwards.

  • Within (Kisi cheez ke andar se)

    Originating from inside something.

    Ex: The water is coming from within the tank. The rat is coming outside from within the jail.

  • Around (Aas paas / Chaaro taraf)

    Ex: Look around, nature is beautiful.

Top Mistakes to Avoid ⚠️

  • 1. Adding '-ly' at the end.

    Adverbs of Place generally DO NOT end in '-ly'. (Adverbs of Manner usually end in -ly like quickly, slowly).

    Wrong: He went outside-ly.

    Right: He went outside.

  • 2. Placement errors (ya to end me ya beech me aate hai).

    Adverbs of place usually come at the END of the sentence, or sometimes in the middle. They rarely come at the very beginning unless for dramatic effect.

    Wrong: Everywhere air is.

    Right: Air is everywhere.

The Lost Wallet

Notice how the adverbs of place (highlighted in blue) give context to WHERE the actions are happening.

Rahul woke up late and started searching everywhere for his wallet. He looked inside his drawer, but it was nowhere to be found.

He walked downstairs to ask his mother. "Mom, is my wallet here?" he asked.

"No beta, I haven't seen it around," she replied from within the kitchen.

Rahul decided to step outside to check his scooter. He walked there quickly. He looked upwards towards the balcony to see if his brother had thrown it. Suddenly, he felt a bulge in his jacket pocket. It was inside his jacket all along!
