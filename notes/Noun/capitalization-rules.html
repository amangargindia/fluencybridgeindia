Capitalization: Proper vs Common Noun

Table of Contents

  • 1. Prerequisites (Kya aana chahiye)
  • 2. Core Concept (Main rule kya hai)
  • 3. Mind Map Rules
  • 4. Top Mistakes People Make

1. Prerequisites

Ye topic samajhne se pehle aapko pata hona chahiye ki Noun (Sangya) kya hota hai. Short mein: Noun kisi bhi insaan, jagah, ya cheez ka naam hota hai.

2. Core Concept

English likhte waqt sabse badi confusion hoti hai ki kaunsa word bade akshar (Capital letter) se shuru karna hai aur kaunsa chhote (small letter) se. Yahan do main concept aate hain:

Common Noun

Aam naam. Kisi aam cheez ya jagah ko bolte hain. Isko humesha small letter se likhte hain (jab tak ye sentence ka pehla word na ho).

Example: city, boy, biscuit, car

Proper Noun

Khas naam. Kisi specific (khas) insaan, brand, ya jagah ka naam. Ye humesha Capital letter se shuru hote hain.

Example: Mumbai, Rahul, Parle-G, Honda

3. Mind Map Rules & Examples

  • People (Insaano ke naam) Common: The boy is playing. (Aam ladka)
    Proper: Ramesh is playing. (Khas ladka)
  • Places (Jagaho ke naam) Common: I live in a big city.
    Proper: I live in Delhi.
  • Brands/Organizations Common: I bought a new phone from the bank.
    Proper: I bought an iPhone using HDFC Bank card.
  • Days, Months, Holidays Humesha Capital honge: Monday, January, Diwali, Eid.
    But Seasons (Mausam) small hote hain: summer, winter.

4. Top Mistakes People Make

Mistake 1: Professions ko capital karna.

He is a Doctor.

He is a doctor.

Kyun? Doctor ek aam profession (common noun) hai, kisi ka naam nahi. Lekin agar title ki tarah use ho "Doctor Sharma", tab Capital aayega.

Mistake 2: Family relations ko galat capital karna.

My Mother cooks well.

My mother cooks well.

Kyun? Yahan 'mother' common noun hai. Lekin agar hum seedha naam ki jagah bula rahe hain: "Can you help me, Mother?", tab Capital hoga.

Story Time: Rohan's Journey

Notice how specific names (Rohan, Mumbai, Monday, Rajdhani Express) are Capitalized, while general terms (village, city, day, train) are in lowercase.

Rohan lived in a small village. For a long time, he wanted to move to a big city. Finally, in the month of January, he packed his bags.

He decided to travel to Mumbai. He bought a ticket for the Rajdhani Express, which is a very fast train. The journey started on Monday morning.

During the trip, he felt hungry. He bought some biscuits. Specifically, he bought Parle-G and a bottle of Kinley water.

When he reached the station, he took an auto-rickshaw to his new office. The company he joined was Tata Consultancy Services. Rohan was very happy. He celebrated by eating at a famous restaurant called Karim's during the Diwali holidays.
