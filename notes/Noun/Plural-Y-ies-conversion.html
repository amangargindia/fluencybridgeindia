2. The Rule: Words Ending in 'Y'

Jab koi english word 'y' par end hota hai aur humein usko plural (bahuvachan) banana hota hai, toh do alag cases aate hain. Yeh completely is baat par depend karta hai ki 'y' se theek pehle wala letter kya hai.

Rule 1 Consonant + Y

Agar 'y' se pehle koi consonant (a, e, i, o, u ke alawa) hai, toh 'y' ko hata kar ies laga do.

  • Baby ➔ Babies (b is consonant)
  • City ➔ Cities (t is consonant)
  • Story ➔ Stories (r is consonant)
Aisa kyun hota hai? (Logic) English pronunciation mein consonants ki bheed (cluster) ek saath bolna mushkil hota hai. 'y' ek vowel sound (/i/ ya /aɪ/) deta hai. Ise likhne me 'ies' se replace karne par word ka syllable structure aur bolne ka flow clear rehta hai.
Rule 2 Vowel + Y

Agar 'y' se pehle koi vowel (a, e, i, o, u) hai, toh simply s laga do. Kuch aur change nahi hoga.

  • Boy ➔ Boys ('o' is vowel)
  • Day ➔ Days ('a' is vowel)
  • Key ➔ Keys ('e' is vowel)
Aisa kyun hota hai? (Logic) Jab 'y' se pehle vowel aata hai (jaise 'ay', 'ey', 'oy'), toh dono milkar ek solid vowel sound (diphthong) banate hain. Iske aage sirf 's' lagane se koi awkward consonant sound nahi banta, isliye yaha 'y' ko change karne ki zarurat nahi hoti.

3. Nuances & Proper Nouns (Exceptions)

Kya kabhi kisi consonant + Y wale word mein sirf 's' lagta hai? Yes! Proper nouns (kisi insaan ya jagah ke special naam) mein spelling change nahi karte.

  • Mere class mein do Mary padhti hain ➔ There are two Marys in my class. (Maries NAHI hoga kyunki yeh ek naam hai).
  • Main Germany ke kayi shehro me gaya hun ➔ I visited two Germanys. (Historical context me, East and West Germany).

4. Top Mistakes People Make

  • ❌ Monkeies | ✅ Monkeys
    Log sochte hain har 'y' wale word me 'ies' lagega. Par 'monkey' me 'e' (vowel) hai 'y' se pehle. So just add 's'.
  • ❌ Familys | ✅ Families
    Family me 'l' (consonant) hai. Sirf 's' lagana galat hai, 'ies' chahiye.
  • ❌ Journies | ✅ Journeys
    Safar ko journey kehte hain. 'e' vowel hone ki wajah se sirf 's' lagega.

Learning Through Story

Notice the bold words. Observe how the plural is formed based on the letter before 'Y'.

Rahul lived in one of the busiest cities in India. Like many middle-class families, his family loved going out. During holidays, he had many days off from work.

One evening, Rahul was getting ready for several Diwali parties. He picked up his keys from the table. He needed to buy gifts for his nephews and nieces. He drove his bike past several bakeries to reach the toy shop. The shop was full of soft monkeys, small cars, and other toys for babies.

When he returned home, the ladies in his house were busy arranging sweet trays. The kids were listening to different stories told by their grandfather. Sometimes, managing finances and buying so many gifts created difficulties, but seeing the smile on everyone's face made all the worries fade away.
