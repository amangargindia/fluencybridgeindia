1. Over vs Above (Upar)

Dono ka matlab 'upar' hota hai lekin unka use alag situations mein hota hai.

  • OVER ka use:
    • Covering / Touching: Jab ek cheez dusri ko cover kar rahi ho. (Put a blanket over the sleeping baby.)
    • Directly Vertically Upar: Jab movement ek side se dusri side direct upar ho. (The dog jumped over the fence.)
    • Numbers / Age / Speed: "More than" ke sense mein. (He is over 30 years old. I get over 100 emails daily.)
  • ABOVE ka use:
    • Higher Level: Jab koi cheez kisi level se upar ho par direct sar ke upar na ho. (The mirror is above the sink.)
    • Standards / Measurements: Temperature, sea level, average. (The temperature is above average. The town is 500m above sea level.)
    • In a document: "Pehle bataya gaya" refer karne ke liye. (Please read the paragraph above.)

2. Under vs Below (Neeche)

Dono ka matlab 'neeche' hota hai, yeh Over aur Above ke direct opposites hain.

  • UNDER ka use (Opposite of Over):
    • Covered by / Hidden: Jab koi cheez fully cover ho chuki ho. (The cat is hiding under the bed.)
    • Directly vertically neeche: (We sat under a tree.)
    • Numbers / Age / Rules: "Less than" ke sense mein. (You cannot buy this if you are under 18. Under the new law...)
  • BELOW ka use (Opposite of Above):
    • Lower Level: Jab koi cheez level mein neeche ho, zaroori nahi vertically direct neeche. (They live in the apartment below ours.)
    • Standards / Measurements: Temperature, average se neeche. (It is 10 degrees below freezing. His performance was below average.)
    • In a document: "Neeche dekho". (See the table below.)

Top Mistakes Indian Students Make

  • Mistake: "My marks are under average."
    Correction: "My marks are below average." (Average is a level/standard).
  • Mistake: "Put the jacket above him."
    Correction: "Put the jacket over him." (Jacket covers him).
  • Mistake: "I am below 18."
    Correction: "I am under 18." (Age uses under/over).

Contextual Story: The Village Rescue

It was a rainy night in the small village. The river water level was rapidly rising above the danger mark. The villagers knew that if the water went over the bridge, they would be trapped.

Raju, a young boy under fifteen years old, noticed something strange. He saw a small puppy trapped under a fallen branch near the riverbank. The rain poured over his head as he ran towards the puppy.

The temperature had dropped below normal, making the water freezing cold. He threw his large raincoat over the puppy to keep it warm. A rescue helicopter hovered above them, flashing a bright light over the entire area.

Eventually, a rescuer climbed down a rope. They safely pulled Raju and the puppy up. The dangerous river was now far below them, and Raju felt a wave of relief wash over him.
